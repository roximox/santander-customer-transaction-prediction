## Methodology

Interpretation was restricted to the training partition. Findings were treated
as descriptive signals rather than causal evidence or automatic feature-selection rules.

## Results

The dataset contained 200 anonymous numeric features and a strongly imbalanced
target. Weak pairwise correlations did not eliminate the possibility of useful
nonlinear and multivariate signal. Potential outliers were retained.

## Interpretation

The EDA supported testing tree models because they can represent nonlinear
thresholds and interactions. It did not justify deleting features or unusual values.

## Decision

Retain the complete feature set for baseline tree modeling and use
imbalance-aware evaluation metrics.

## Difficulties

of variables required selective rather than exhaustive visualization.

## Adaptations and deviations from the plan

instead of plotting every possible relationship.

## Rejected approaches

based on univariate differences were rejected.
