## How this log was kept

The entries below are anchored to the commit history of this repository. Each entry states
the commits it corresponds to, and the **Basis for time** field records how the time figure
was arrived at: where a session produced several commits, the figure is the span between the
first and the last; where a session produced a single commit, the figure is an estimate and
is labelled as one.

Work on this package was concentrated into four sessions (13, 18, 20 and 30 August) rather
than spread evenly across the ten-week period. The log reflects that pattern rather than
smoothing it, and the time recorded here is the time that is evidenced. Reading, failed
runs, and meeting attendance that left no commit are not counted, so the recorded total is
lower than the time actually spent.
