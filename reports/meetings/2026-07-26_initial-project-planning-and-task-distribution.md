Work packages were planned to support parallel progress; common evaluation and documentation conventions would be refined later. Responsibilities were distributed across four planned areas, each including an ML contribution: Member 01 — project setup, data engineering and reproducibility, with Logistic Regression; Member 02 — exploratory data analysis, with Random Forest and Extra Trees; Member 03 — feature engineering and dimensionality reduction, with PCA, feature selection, and Logistic Regression; Member 04 — model optimization and evaluation, with Gradient Boosting / HistGradientBoosting. Common methodology and reproducibility will be maintained across the team. Important scientific results and final model selection will be discussed jointly at a later stage; detailed implementation will follow the initial planning phase.

Member 02 will plan exploratory data analysis, descriptive statistics, visualization and data understanding, correlation and variable analysis, and tree-based models. Member 03 will plan feature engineering, PCA, feature selection, reduced-feature modeling, and later comparison of reduced and original feature representations. Member 04 will focus on Gradient Boosting / HistGradientBoosting and will later work on model optimization, diagnostic evaluation, and model comparison. All members will participate in team meetings, code review, personal logbook documentation, scientific discussion and interpretation, portfolio preparation, and final-presentation preparation.

The team must avoid data leakage and premature use of the reserved test set. No concrete technical blocker had appeared because development had not started.
